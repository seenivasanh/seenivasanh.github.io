About

Background & Work

I am an independent researcher and consultant working at the intersection of quantum computing and computational chemistry. My work focuses on developing and applying quantum algorithms to tackle challenging problems in electronic structure of molecules and materials.

With a background spanning computational chemistry, chemical physics and quantum computing, I collaborate with academic groups and industry partners to bridge fundamental research and practical quantum computing.

I am open to research collaborations, consulting engagements, and advisory roles in quantum computing and computational chemistry.

Research

Research Interests

⚛️

Quantum Algorithms for Chemistry

Variational quantum eigensolvers, quantum phase estimation, and hybrid classical-quantum methods for electronic structure problems. Near-term and fault-tolerant resource estimation.

🔬

Molecular Reaction Dynamics

Quantum wave-packet dynamics, molecular reaction dynamics, reaction pathways, and excited-state phenomena.

🧮

Quantum Error Mitigation

Techniques to extract reliable computational chemistry results from near-term noisy quantum hardware.

💎

Heterogeneous Catalysis

Developing quantum algorithms to model catalytic reactions at surfaces, with applications to sustainable chemical processes and industrial catalysis.
