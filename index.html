Background & Work

Download CV

I am an independent researcher and consultant working at the intersection of quantum computing and computational chemistry. My work focuses on developing and applying quantum algorithms to tackle challenging problems in molecular simulation, electronic structure, and materials discovery.

With a background spanning quantum information theory and chemical physics, I collaborate with academic groups and industry partners to bridge fundamental research and practical quantum advantage.

I am open to research collaborations, consulting engagements, and advisory roles in quantum computing and computational science.

Research

Research Interests

⚛️

Quantum Algorithms for Chemistry

Variational quantum eigensolvers, quantum phase estimation, and hybrid classical-quantum methods for electronic structure problems.

🔬

Molecular Simulation

Quantum-enhanced simulation of molecular dynamics, reaction pathways, and excited-state phenomena beyond classical tractability.

🧮

Quantum Error Mitigation

Techniques to extract reliable computational chemistry results from near-term noisy quantum hardware.

💎

Materials Discovery

Applying quantum computation to the design of novel materials with targeted electronic, magnetic, and catalytic properties.
