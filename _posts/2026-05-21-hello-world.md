Welcome to the first post on this site.

I've been meaning to start a writing habit for a while — not academic papers, but shorter, more informal pieces. Things I'm thinking about, papers I found interesting, computational experiments, half-formed ideas that don't deserve a full publication but are worth writing down.

The plan is to post something here roughly once a week. It might be:

- A summary of a paper I read that week
- A technical note on quantum computing or computational chemistry
- A reflection on something in the research process
- An explanation of a concept I've been trying to understand better

The posts will vary in length and depth. Some will be quick — a few paragraphs pointing at something interesting. Others might go deeper.

If you have thoughts or want to discuss anything, reach me by [email](/index.html#contact) or on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/seenivasan-hariharan/). I'm always happy to talk.
